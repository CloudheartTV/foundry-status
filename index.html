The GM

Hi, I'm
Cloudheart

I'm a tabletop RPG game master and live streamer running six simultaneous campaigns across wildly different systems and settings — from post-apocalyptic Georgia to Imperial Tamriel, from a JRPG isekai to the frontiers of space of 2310.

All sessions are run live on FoundryVTT, streamed on Twitch, and archived on YouTube. Seats are open — come play, or pull up a chair and watch.

As for me? I'm a lot of things. Son, Brother, Friend.

>I've always been enamored with computers and putting things together. Fantasy and Science Fiction are my go-tos when I am reading, and SNES and PS1 un-voiced text-based RPGs are the reason I learned to read.

I'm a fairly open person, and I've had the blessing in life to experience the love of friends, family and the depth and myriad of knowledge, mystery, joy and pain this life has to offer and, I think, you have to live first if you want to make and say something about life. I find, at the end of the day, though, I was never much concerned with WHO made my media save for learning about a new perspective from them to view the world.

No, what's more important to me is that I create something that actually speaks to you. Like a random name on the back of a good game box or in the scrolling list of credits, let me fade to the back and tell me if you enjoyed what was made.

Ultimately, I thank you all — first to my family and friends who helped me learn to live and see things differently, and enriched my life and enabled me to pursue this work.

And thank you, players and viewers, for existing and wanting to experience these creations, and who honestly give me a reason to create and make the end result so much better than I could have alone.
